Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Analytica MAS for Accounting and Auditing LLC ("Company", "we", "us") operates Analytica-Mas QuickBooks (the "App"). This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address.
  • QuickBooks data: with your authorization, accounting data from your QuickBooks Online account (such as customers, invoices, transactions, and financial reports) needed to provide the App's analytics.
  • Authentication tokens issued by Intuit to access your QuickBooks data.
  • Basic technical data such as log and usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve the App, generate your reports and analytics, secure your account, and communicate with you about the service.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or QuickBooks data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to operate the App (such as hosting and email delivery), under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We protect data using encryption in transit (HTTPS) and appropriate access controls. Authentication tokens are stored securely.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service. When you disconnect the App from QuickBooks, we stop accessing your data and delete your stored QuickBooks data and tokens within a reasonable period. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us.

8. Children

The App is not intended for individuals under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
